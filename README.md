# The Essence of Life · 生命的本质

> Life is more than carbon chemistry. Life may be the universe organizing information against entropy — and perhaps beginning to understand itself.

An epic, cinematic, single-page exploration of the deepest question in existence: **what is life?** It answers through nine converging lenses — biology, information theory, thermodynamics, mathematics, evolution, consciousness, artificial intelligence, cosmology and philosophy — wrapped in a living WebGL universe.

## Experience

- **Living cosmic background** — a Three.js starfield + spiral galaxy + custom GLSL nebula whose colour migrates through the journey as you scroll (cosmos-blue → life-green → consciousness-violet → civilisation-gold → luminous white).
- **What Is Life?** — four perspectives, each with its own real-time visualization: cell division (mitosis), DNA-as-code streams, a negentropy entropy field, and a golden-ratio phyllotaxis + Game of Life.
- **Consciousness** — an interactive neural network you stimulate with your cursor; signals cascade and nodes fire.
- **AI & Life** — a "future evolutionary documentary": digital genome, carbon-mind vs silicon-mind comparison, and frontiers of artificial life.
- **Civilization** — a scroll-scrubbed timeline from particles to cosmic consciousness.
- **Ultimate Questions** — five questions and a cosmic finale.

## Stack

- **Next.js 14** (App Router, TypeScript, `src/app`)
- **Three.js / WebGL** — GLSL nebula + particle galaxy/starfield
- **Canvas 2D** — DNA helix, cell division, entropy field, cellular automata, Fibonacci spiral, code stream, neural field (all hand-written, dependency-free)
- **Framer Motion** — scroll reveals & finale
- **GSAP ScrollTrigger** — the civilization timeline spine
- **Tailwind CSS** — Cormorant Garamond · Space Grotesk · Spectral · JetBrains Mono · Noto Serif SC
